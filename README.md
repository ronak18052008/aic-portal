# 🚀 AIC Portal — Academia–Industry Collaboration Portal

> **Smart India Hackathon 2026 | Problem Statement 26044**

AIC Portal is a centralized Academia–Industry Collaboration platform designed to bridge the gap between **students, academicians, institutions, and industries** through skill mapping, learning, internships, placements, talent discovery, and industry collaboration.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

There is a significant gap between the skills acquired by students in academic institutions and the competencies expected by industries.

Students often struggle to:

- Identify the skills required for their desired careers
- Understand their current skill gaps
- Find relevant internships and job opportunities
- Access industry-relevant learning opportunities
- Showcase their skills, projects, certifications, and achievements

At the same time, industries face challenges in finding candidates with the right skill sets, while academicians have limited access to industry internships, training, research, and collaboration opportunities.

AIC Portal addresses these challenges through a unified digital ecosystem.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

AIC Portal connects:

- 👨‍🎓 Students
- 🏫 Institutions
- 👨‍🏫 Academicians
- 🏢 Industries
- 💼 Recruiters

The platform supports the complete journey:

**Assess → Identify → Improve → Match → Collaborate**

Our goal is to connect academic learning with real-world industry requirements and create a continuous ecosystem for skill development, internships, placements, and collaboration.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 AI Skill Intelligence

The platform analyzes student skills and helps identify:

- Technical skills
- Soft skills
- Strengths
- Skill gaps
- Career-oriented requirements

---

### 📊 Skill Assessment & Skill Gap Analysis

Students can evaluate their technical and soft skills through assessments.

The system helps identify skill gaps by comparing the student's current capabilities with industry requirements.

---

### 🗺️ Career Roadmap

Students can receive a personalized roadmap to improve their skills and prepare for their desired career paths.

The roadmap can focus on short-term and long-term development goals.

---

### 📚 Personalized Learning

Students can discover relevant:

- Learning resources
- Training programs
- Certification courses
- Workshops
- Industry-relevant learning opportunities

---

### 💼 Internship & Job Opportunities

Industries can publish:

- Internships
- Projects
- Apprenticeships
- Entry-level jobs
- Training programs
- Workshops
- Mentorship opportunities

Students can search, apply, and track opportunities through the platform.

---

### 🏢 Industry Challenges

Industries can publish real-world challenges for students.

Students can:

1. Understand the problem
2. Develop a solution
3. Submit their solution
4. Gain practical experience
5. Demonstrate their skills

This helps connect academic knowledge with real-world problem solving.

---

### 🔍 Talent Discovery

The Talent Discovery module helps industries discover suitable student candidates based on their skills and requirements.

This promotes **skill-based recruitment** rather than relying only on traditional academic qualifications.

---

### 📋 Applicant Tracking System

Recruiters can manage candidates through an ATS-style recruitment workflow.

Recruiters can:

- View applicants
- Evaluate candidates
- Shortlist candidates
- Track applications
- Manage recruitment activities

---

### 🪪 AIC Passport

AIC Passport provides students with a unified digital profile containing information such as:

- Skills
- Certifications
- Projects
- Internships
- Assessments
- Achievements
- Career information

It provides students with a structured digital representation of their career development.

---

### 👨‍🏫 Academia & Faculty Collaboration

The platform can support academicians through opportunities such as:

- Faculty internships
- Industrial training
- Faculty Development Programs
- Consultancy opportunities
- Research collaboration
- Industry mentorship
- Workshops
- Guest lectures

---

### 📈 Dashboards & Analytics

Institutions and other stakeholders can use dashboards to monitor:

- Student skill development
- Internship participation
- Placement progress
- Industry requirements
- Recruitment activities
- Skill demand trends
